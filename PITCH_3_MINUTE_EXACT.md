## TIPS FOR DELIVERY

### Tone
- Confident but not arrogant
- Energetic when results appear
- Clear and deliberate with technical terms

### Pacing
- Speak normally during face detection (fast)
- Slow down during OSINT search (emphasize "real")
- Get excited when results appear ("Look at this!")
- Firm and clear during summary

### Body Language
- Point at screen during key moments
- Make eye contact when explaining concepts
- Show genuine surprise when results appear
- Stand tall during final summary

### Key Phrases to Emphasize
1. "No mock data" - say firmly
2. "Real web search" - slow down
3. "Look at these URLs" - point excitedly
4. "Blockchain proof" - slow and serious
5. "All 6 requirements met" - confident

### Technical Terms (Say Clearly)
- "Biometric embedding" (bi-oh-MET-rik)
- "512-dimensional" (five-twelve dimensional)
- "Perceptual hash" (per-SEP-tual)
- "Merkle tree" (MER-kul)
- "OSINT" (OH-sint)
- "Provenance" (PROV-en-ance)
